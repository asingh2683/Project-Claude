Donation receipts

For a single gift of $250 or more, the IRS requires a written acknowledgment from the charity before you claim a deduction. If you need a receipt, send us the date and amount of your gift.

Request a receipt

What a receipt shows

  • The name of the organization
  • The amount of a cash gift, or a description of a non-cash gift
  • Whether you received goods or services in return, and their estimated value
