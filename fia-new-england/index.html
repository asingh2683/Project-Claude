Who we are

One community, many traditions

FIA New England is an umbrella non-profit that unites Indian American individuals, families and community organizations across the region. We celebrate the diversity of India's languages, faiths and cultures, and we work to strengthen the bonds between the Indian American community and the broader New England community we call home.

Run entirely by volunteers, we organize signature cultural festivals, support charitable causes, empower youth, and give our community a voice in civic life.
