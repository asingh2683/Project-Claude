Welcome to FIA New England

We are a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization celebrating Indian heritage and serving communities across Massachusetts, Connecticut, Rhode Island, New Hampshire, Vermont and Maine.

Our work runs from health camps and food drives to scholarships and the India Day Parade, and it is open to neighbors of every background.

More about us

Our mission

Our mission is to strengthen communities across New England through service, culture, education and humanitarian care.

Community service

Meeting everyday needs, from food drives to support for veterans.

Cultural engagement

Sharing India's traditions with every generation and every neighbor.

Education

Helping students learn, lead and reach their goals.

Humanitarian initiatives

Caring for health and for people in times of need.
